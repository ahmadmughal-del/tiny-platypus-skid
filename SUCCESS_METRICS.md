Activation & Engagement 
● Sprint Completion Rate: The percentage of users who start a guided sprint  
● Time-to-Value: The average time it takes for a new user to generate their 
first "Forged Artifact."  
● Feature Adoption Rate: The percentage of users who actively engage with 
the core tools,  
2. Conversion 
● Artefact-to-Trial Conversion Rate: percentage of users who, upon viewing 
their completed "Forged Artifact," click to "Start 7-Day Free Trial" rather 
than continuing without exporting. 
● Trial-to-Paid Conversion Rate: The percentage of users who transition 
from a free trial to a full, paid subscription.  
3. Retention & Growth 
● Repeat Sprint Rate: The percentage of paying users who return to 
complete a second, third, and subsequent sprints.  
● Active User Engagement: The number of monthly or weekly active users 
(MAU/WAU), particularly those who are engaging with the "My Forge" 
section and their "Cognitive Fingerprint."
