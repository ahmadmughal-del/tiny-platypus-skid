We're getting good at prompt engineering but losing our edge in critical, divergent thinking. The unsettling feeling that our best ideas are borrowed, not earned, leads to professional insecurity and, ultimately, a loss of competitive advantage. ### 2. MVP Scope – Must-Have Features for Launch

The following features represent the Minimum Viable Product (MVP) and are essential for the initial launch:

1.  **Landing Page & Onboarding:** A single, high-impact landing page that drives users to a one-click sign-up with a Google account. The onboarding should lead directly to the Core Workspace.
2.  **Core Strategic Sprint:** The core 3-step workflow must be fully functional, with the canvas remaining fully editable throughout the Unpack and Stress-Test phases.
    *   **Unpack:** The ability for users to enter a chaotic "thought dump" via text or voice. The AI must be able to automatically organize this into a basic set of digital notes. Users can then freely edit and refine this content, including rewriting, adding, deleting, merging, splitting, or reordering notes and sections.
    *   **Stress-Test:** The AI Coach can take on a single, key persona to challenge the user's ideas with strategic, open-ended questions. During this phase, users retain full control to edit their own responses to the AI's questions and further refine the canvas content.
    *   **Finalize:** The ability to instantly transform the canvas into a polished, executive-level, one-page report. The report will be generated based on the final, user-edited content on the canvas, ensuring the user always has ultimate control over the output.
3.  **Source & Fact-Check:** The AI's ability to pull in and cite a limited number of external, relevant sources (e.g., from major publications or historical events) to support its coaching. A simple, in-app pop-over must show the source, enabling the user to verify it.
4.  **Paywall & Conversion:** The modal that appears when a user tries to export their first report. It must clearly articulate the value of the paid tier and offer a seamless, low-friction entry into a free trial (e.g., a 7-day trial).
5.  **User Dashboard:** A simple "My Forge" page that shows a history of all completed reports. This serves as the first iteration of the "Cognitive Fingerprint," providing a record of the user's progress and a reason to return to the product.

### 3. User Stories / Workflows

*   **As a professional under pressure,** I can start a session with a single, clear prompt, so I don't get stuck on a blank page.
*   **As a time-constrained manager,** I can quickly get my chaotic thoughts out of my head and see them instantly organized into a structured strategic framework by the AI.
*   **As a professional preparing for a high-stakes meeting,** I can have the AI rigorously challenge my proposal from different perspectives, so my final strategy is bulletproof.
*   **As a leader who values evidence,** I can see external sources and data seamlessly integrated and verified on my canvas, so my arguments are grounded in fact.
*   **As a leader who needs to present a polished plan,** I can instantly transform my messy canvas into a professional, executive-level report, so my ideas are taken seriously.
*   **As a professional committed to growth,** I can view a data-driven "cognitive fingerprint" that shows how my strategic thinking is improving over time, so I can confidently track my development.

### 4. Success Metrics

We will measure the success of the product through the following key metrics:

#### 1. Activation & Engagement

*   **Sprint Completion Rate:** The percentage of users who start a guided sprint and complete all three steps (Unpack, Stress-Test, Finalize).
*   **Time-to-Value:** The average time it takes for a new user to generate their first "Forged Artifact" (completed report).
*   **Feature Adoption Rate:** The percentage of users who actively engage with the core tools, specifically utilizing the editing capabilities during Unpack and Stress-Test, and interacting with the Source & Fact-Check feature.

#### 2. Conversion

*   **Artefact-to-Trial Conversion Rate:** The percentage of users who, upon viewing their completed "Forged Artifact," click to "Start 7-Day Free Trial" rather than continuing without exporting.
*   **Trial-to-Paid Conversion Rate:** The percentage of users who transition from a free trial to a full, paid subscription.

#### 3. Retention & Growth

*   **Repeat Sprint Rate:** The percentage of paying users who return to complete a second, third, and subsequent sprints within a given period.
*   **Active User Engagement:** The number of monthly or weekly active users (MAU/WAU), particularly those who are engaging with the "My Forge" section and their "Cognitive Fingerprint."
